The Process

3 R's — Reuse. Refurbish. Recycle.

Every device that comes through Refurbicx follows this rule: Reuse what works, Refurbish what doesn't, Recycle what's truly done. Nothing goes straight to the landfill. See the full process →

R

Reuse What Works

About 4 in 5 devices that meet us, still power on and function. Those go straight back into circulation — cleaned, data-wiped, and resold into the local Marketplace.

R

Refurbish What Doesn't

Cracked screens, dead batteries, failing ports — fixable problems get fixed. Component-level repair using parts harvested from devices that can't be saved and third party suppliers.

R

Recycle What's Done

When a device has truly reached the end, it's broken down responsibly so the raw materials inside — copper, gold, rare earths — go back into the economy instead of the ground.

Nothing Out. Nothing wasted. Everything finds a second life.
