Our story

Throwing away a computer is about as crazy as throwing away gold.

Here's why Refurbicx exists, straight from the person who started it.

PHOTO PLACEHOLDER — TO BE REPLACED
Aiden
FOUNDER, REFURBICX

This exists because throwing away computers was about as crazy as throwing away gold. Hi, I'm Aiden, a 16-year-old entrepreneur in the U.S. trying to change the game in tech.

Most of the tech you buy becomes "obsolete" and gets thrown out. Tech companies have convinced us to keep buying the latest and allegedly greatest — but they never told us how to get rid of our old devices. So most of them end up in the garage, joining the ranks of our other waste in landfills, where they'll sit for centuries. That was the best mindset we had just a few decades ago — insane. This is not how it needs to be. We need to rethink how tech actually gets disposed of.

When I was in my early teens, I'd get my hands on computers and functional tech from local recycling centers, friends and family, dumpsters, transfer stations, and even my neighbor's garage can. What surprised me was that almost 80% of the devices I found worked — as soon as I hit the power button. It hit me then: this is a goldmine, a blue ocean of possibility that no one had ever taken a stab at. That's what brings me here now.

~$70 billion
in precious metals thrown out every year in e-waste — and the collective value of four decades of discarded electronics sits in the trillions.

I believe taking part in this e-waste issue unlocks a much bigger message about society — we need to rethink what we call "trash." Electronics are now the fastest-growing waste stream in the world. Literal gold is being thrown out and no one is giving it a second thought.

Refurbicx aims to change the game for good — building a north star for the tech giants and showing them the best approach to this ever-growing problem. Using our RRR process and new technologies of our own, like AI and machine learning, we plan to take on the piles of old devices and extract value from each and every one of them. Nothing will end up in a landfill. Everything will make its way to a new life, providing value to something entirely different.

Our goal isn't to pocket billions of dollars. It's to prove to the world that an environmentally responsible company can turn otherwise-junk into a profitable, beneficial business — for the economy and the planet. This is a global effort, and we plan to lead it.

That's my aim with Refurbicx, and how I plan to make it happen.

— Aiden Founder, Refurbicx
What we believe

We're not building another tech company. We're building the a system of oppertunity.

Refurbicx is the system that turns neglect into value. These three ideas are what that system runs on.

PRINCIPLE

Reuse beats recycling.

Recycling recovers materials. Reuse keeps a device's embedded energy, labor, and materials intact. We default to "can this be saved?" before "what can we extract from this?"

PRINCIPLE

Trust is the actual product.

People won't hand over a device with their data on it unless they trust what happens next. Every intake gets certified data wiping and a paper trail, no exceptions.

PRINCIPLE

Scale feeds our Success.

One household's drawer isn't enough, and neither is one company's fleet refresh. We're building the pipeline to scale, mathcing the size to meet the world's demand.
